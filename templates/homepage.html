🐧 Penguin Language Learning App

Welcome! This app helps you learn new languages using short quizzes.

How to Play

  1. Click Start Learning
  2. Choose a language and difficulty
  3. Answer 5 multiple-choice questions
  4. Submit your answers
  5. Read your results

Difficulty System

  • No Brainer – Very easy
  • Easy
  • Medium
  • Hard
  • Impossible – Very hard

Your difficulty will automatically adjust based on how well you do:

  • 0–1 correct → easier
  • 2–3 correct → same
  • 4–5 correct → harder
